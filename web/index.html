How it works

  1. Latency: 20 tiny requests measure ping and jitter.
  2. Download & upload: parallel streams run for about 8 seconds each against Cloudflare's global speed test network, with a fallback on this site's own edge servers.
  3. Verdict: results are compared with your plan and a ready-to-post message is drafted for your provider.

Privacy: no accounts, no tracking, no server-side storage. Your results and plan stay in your browser. Strict security headers (CSP, HSTS) are enforced on every page.
